# Maintainers

Maintainers review pull requests, triage issues, cut releases, and guide
the project's direction. They also keep the community welcoming, as the
[code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) describes.

## Current maintainers

- `jadidbourbaki`

## Getting in touch

Questions and ideas go in a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/jadidbourbaki/bobcat/issues).
Vulnerabilities go through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have reviewed and landed changes over time are welcome
to ask about becoming a maintainer in an issue.
